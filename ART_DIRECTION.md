# Industrial fieldwork art direction

The design uses Marathon as a mood and graphic-design reference: compressed typography, utilitarian labels, acid-green accents, orange markers, and grounded industrial environments. The hero is original generated artwork, not a game screenshot or licensed Marathon asset.

Reference: https://www.marathonthegame.com/

## Generated artwork

- Final asset: `assets/images/industrial-chamber.png`
- Method: built-in Imagegen tool (not the CLI).
- The original generation is retained in the Codex generated-images directory; a copy is bundled with this project.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide cinematic background photograph for a creative technologist's dark industrial science-fiction portfolio. Create an original brutalist computational research chamber, ominous and tactile, inspired by analog industrial design and the dark visual mood of Marathon. A towering worn graphite-black rectangular machine monolith stands on the RIGHT THIRD, with stacked rectangular modules, exposed mechanical seams, subtle stenciled geometric symbols (no readable text), and a narrow acid-yellow-green luminous strip cut into its front face. Behind it, immense cold concrete walls and a few thin vertical utilities vanish into dark green-black fog. A small hard orange indicator near the monolith base; damp rough floor reflects only a little light. The LEFT HALF must remain mostly very dark negative space with subtle atmospheric texture so large white website typography can be overlaid there. Wide 16:9 landscape, cinematic close-medium architectural view, harsh directional lighting, desaturated near-monochrome film grain, precise sharp mechanical details, restrained toxic green highlights. Mood: brooding, abandoned, intelligent, mysterious, grounded, premium art direction. No planets, no stars, no spheres, no orbital rings, no outer space, no characters, no weapons, no logos, no watermarks, no text, no UI. Do not reproduce any specific game asset.
