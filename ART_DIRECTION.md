# Signal / independent creative practice

An original cyberpunk identity for Isaac Nino. Graphite and icy blue remain the foundation, with restrained signal-red details. IBM Plex Mono typography throughout, clipped corners, orthogonal traces, subtle scan lines, and an asymmetric composition give the page a harder technical character. The aesthetic takes a broad cue from Cyberpunk 2077 without using its assets, logo, signature typography, or fictional terminology.

The previous circular discipline controls are replaced by a signal switchboard. Four selectable channels each redraw a distinct, original SVG practice map: systems, applied AI, interaction, and web. Each channel now contains four selectable layers with a detailed diagram, a short explanation, and three concrete details. Systems reveals intake, rules, DOCX assembly, and refinement. Applied AI explores a creative brief, prompt matrix, generation, and creative review. Interaction changes between gallery browsing, selection, focused media, and return. Web shows content hierarchy, visual tone, responsive arrangement, and contact. Each study links to its related project. These are editorial process studies grounded in the portfolio descriptions; no fake performance metrics are shown. Keyboard-operable buttons expose their selected state, preserve focus, and remember the last layer per channel.

The portrait uses the existing photograph with a CSS blue monochrome and scan-line treatment. No replacement identity or generated portrait is used. The work section retains actual project screenshots and natural scrolling, now with angular frames and vertical image transitions. The contact symbol is a geometric arrow, and the interlude stays dark.

Decorative movement follows scroll and pointer input. Channel transitions are brief and event-driven. System reduced-motion support and the persistent motion control remain available. Mobile uses a two-column channel selector and inline project imagery.

Project content remains in content/projects.json. Older unused artwork remains available in repository history and is not referenced by this interface.

Monospace headings are resized for their wider proportions, including small-screen layouts. Glitches are localized, brief color separations and horizontal signal tears tied to deliberate moments: the opening headline, channel changes, and visible project changes. Body text stays steady. The visual text copies are hidden from assistive technology, with a per-element cooldown and immediate cancellation when motion is paused.

The hero reads “Considered digital experiences.” Its positioning centers Isaac’s deep agency experience, creative judgment, and a tinkerer’s curiosity. Supporting copy connects strategy, design, and technology from the first conversation to the final detail. On phones, detailed diagrams retain a readable canvas and pan to the selected layer; explanatory text remains in the normal page flow.

A finite ISAAC NINO acquisition screen introduces the portfolio with oversized monospace type, cyan/coral sliced offsets, scan lines, and four readiness checks. The reveal lifts the overlay into the hero; it does not add ongoing flashing. The intro follows the existing motion preference, supports an immediate skip, and fails open if loading stalls.

The hero register reads “Strategic perspective. Personal attention.” The practice statements form a seamless running banner, pausing on hover and following motion preferences. Brief headline glitches recur every 3.2 seconds while the headline is visible, with no timer running when it is offscreen or paused.
